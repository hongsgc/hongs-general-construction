Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026. This page explains what information hongsgcpl.com, the website of Hong's General Construction & Plumbing in Federal Way, Washington, collects and how it is used.

The estimate form

When you send the estimate form, we receive the name, phone number, email, city, type of job and message you enter. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which emails it to estimates@hongsgcpl.com. We use it to reply about your project.

If you call or email us instead, we receive your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see how people find the site (for example, from a search or a link). Google sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view. It does not receive what you type into the estimate form. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Microsoft Clarity

We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay, to improve the site. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies. The estimate form is masked, so what you type into it is not recorded. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address when you visit, to keep the service running and secure.

Questions

To ask what information we have about you, or to have it deleted, call 206-227-2275 or email estimates@hongsgcpl.com.
